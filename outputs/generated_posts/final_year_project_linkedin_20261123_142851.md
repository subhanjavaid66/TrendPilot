## Caption
Navigating a final year project can feel like scaling a mountain – exhilarating, but demanding serious preparation.

Looking back on my own experience, the single piece of advice I wish someone had hammered home before starting my final year project is this: *Prioritize ruthless scoping.* It’s incredibly tempting to expand your project’s ambitions as you delve deeper, driven by a desire to truly explore the topic. However, this often leads to scope creep, unrealistic timelines, and ultimately, burnout.

Instead, force yourself to identify the *core* question you're trying to answer and build your project around delivering a robust, insightful response to *that* question alone. Focus on quality over quantity – a well-executed, narrowly defined project is infinitely more valuable than a sprawling, half-finished one.

This discipline wasn’t just about managing time; it was about maintaining a sense of control and achieving a truly satisfying outcome.

What’s the most valuable piece of advice you received during your final year project journey?
