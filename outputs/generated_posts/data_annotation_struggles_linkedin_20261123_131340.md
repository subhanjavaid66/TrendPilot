Data annotation struggles are surprisingly common, and often stem from ambiguity. It’s easy to assume a straightforward definition, but AI models crave precision. A slightly off-center image, a low-resolution screenshot – suddenly, your carefully crafted “dog” label is being challenged by a diligent algorithm.

Clear, detailed guidelines are absolutely essential. Spend the time upfront to define edge cases and provide examples. It’s far easier to prevent confusion than to troubleshoot it later.

What strategies do you find most effective for ensuring consistency in your data annotation teams?
