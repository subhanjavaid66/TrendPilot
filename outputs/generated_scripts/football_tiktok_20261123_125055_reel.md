# Reel Script: Football (TikTok)

Hook (0-3s): “Don’t just dream it. Build it.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up shot of hands meticulously practicing football drills – passing, catching, kicking – sweat glistening. | Voiceover: “Legend says the greatest players aren’t born. They’re forged.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Montage of athletes, young and older, intensely focused on training – running, tackling, studying plays. Fast cuts, energetic music. | Voiceover: “It’s the hours spent perfecting every move, pushing past the limits.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Slow-motion shot of a player making a crucial tackle, followed by a determined look. | Voiceover: “It's the relentless pursuit of excellence, the unwavering commitment to the game.”

Closing line: “What are *you* building today?”
