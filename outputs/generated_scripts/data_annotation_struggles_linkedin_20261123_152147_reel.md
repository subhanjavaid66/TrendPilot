# Reel Script: Data annotation struggles (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “Is your data annotation project feeling… eternal?”

Scene 1 | Visual: A lone, weary-looking person hunched over a computer, meticulously drawing polygons on a screen with a stylus. Ancient-looking scroll scrolls endlessly behind them. | Voiceover: “For centuries, scribes toiled, endlessly refining shapes, chasing the perfect polygon. A Sisyphean task.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick cuts of someone intensely drawing polygons, then deleting them, then drawing them again – all with increasing frustration. | Voiceover: “It's the same today. Hours spent on tiny adjustments, feeling like you’re fighting a losing battle.”

Scene 3 | Visual: The person from Scene 1 sighs dramatically, then a bright, modern interface appears showing automated annotation tools. | Voiceover: “Don’t be a myth. Let intelligent tools handle the grunt work.”

Closing line: “Ready to break the cycle? Let’s talk about smarter annotation.”
