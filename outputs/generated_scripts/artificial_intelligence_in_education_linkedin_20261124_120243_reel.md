# Reel Script: Artificial Intelligence in Education (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “The future of education is collaborative.”

Scene 1 | Visual: A split screen – one side shows a teacher working with a small group of students, the other side shows an AI tutor interacting with a single student on a tablet.
| Voiceover: “The narrative that AI will replace teachers is a myth. It’s about augmenting their abilities.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Close-up shot of a student struggling with a math problem, then a quick transition to the AI tutor instantly providing targeted support and explaining the concept clearly.
| Voiceover: “AI’s true potential unlocks personalized learning experiences, like immediate, targeted support for students.”

Scene 3 | Visual: A teacher smiling, engaging in a discussion with students, fostering critical thinking.
| Voiceover: “Freeing educators to focus on what matters most: developing critical thinking, creativity, and social-emotional skills.”

Closing line: “Let’s build a smarter, more personalized learning journey – together.”
