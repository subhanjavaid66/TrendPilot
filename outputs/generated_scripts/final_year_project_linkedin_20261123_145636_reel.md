# Reel Script: Final year project (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “Forget the ‘brilliant idea’ myth.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up shot of hands meticulously sketching diagrams, then transitioning to a clean spreadsheet. | Voiceover: “The reality of a final year project is often about relentless execution. It’s about refining, iterating, and pushing through the details.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Montage of shots: someone debugging code, someone carefully assembling a prototype, someone presenting data visually. | Voiceover: “It’s about mastering the process – research, testing, documentation, and presenting a polished, demonstrable outcome.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Shot of a graduate smiling confidently, holding a completed project. | Voiceover: “Don’t underestimate the power of disciplined work. A well-executed project speaks volumes.”

Closing line: “Invest your time in meticulous preparation – it’s the key to success.”
