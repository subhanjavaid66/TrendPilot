# Reel Script: Software Engineering is dead or not (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “The craftsman is fading.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Slow-motion shot of a skilled artisan (woodworker, blacksmith) meticulously working on a complex project – perhaps a detailed clock or intricate furniture. The lighting is warm and focused.
| Voiceover: “For centuries, mastery demanded deep understanding and precise execution.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Transition to a modern software engineer collaborating in a bright, open-plan office – coding on a screen, whiteboard with diagrams.
| Voiceover: “Today, the tools change rapidly. But the fundamental challenge remains: translating complex needs into robust, scalable solutions.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Close-up on the engineer’s face, thoughtful expression.
| Voiceover: “Software engineering isn’t about the *tool*, it’s about the *skill* – bridging the gap between what’s asked for and what truly works.”

Closing line: “What’s the core skill you’re building today?”
