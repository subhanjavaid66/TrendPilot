# Reel Script: Final year project (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “Final year projects: the make-or-break.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up of a student intensely working on a laptop, surrounded by books and notes. | Voiceover: “Looking back, the biggest challenge wasn't the technical hurdles, it was the feeling of being overwhelmed.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick cuts of a student sketching, meeting with a supervisor, and struggling with code. | Voiceover: “I wish someone had told me to break the project down into smaller, manageable milestones. Focus on one step at a time.”

Scene 3 | Visual: The student from Scene 1 now smiling confidently, presenting a completed project. | Voiceover: “Prioritize clear communication with your supervisor and relentlessly track your progress.”

Closing line: “Don't let the enormity of the task paralyze you. Start small, stay focused, and you'll get there.”
