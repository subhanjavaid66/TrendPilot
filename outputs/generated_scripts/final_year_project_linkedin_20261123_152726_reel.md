# Reel Script: Final year project (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “That sinking feeling…”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up shot of a student looking stressed, staring at a laptop screen with complex code. | Voiceover: “The biggest hurdle in a final year project isn’t the technical challenges – it’s the feeling of being completely lost.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick montage – a student meeting with a mentor, sketching ideas in a notebook, collaborating with a small group. | Voiceover: “It’s about seeking guidance, breaking down the project into manageable steps, and building a support system.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Shot of a confident student presenting their project, smiling. | Voiceover: “Overcoming that initial disorientation is the key to not just finishing, but thriving. “

Closing line: “Don’t let the overwhelm win. Take it one step at a time.”
