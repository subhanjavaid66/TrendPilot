# Reel Script: Data annotation struggles (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “Ever feel like your computer is judging you?”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up of a person intensely staring at a computer screen, surrounded by sticky notes with “dog” and “dust bunny” written on them. | Voiceover: "We spent a week arguing with a machine about whether this blurry blob was a dog… or a particularly stubborn dust bunny.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick cuts of increasingly confused faces looking at data annotation interfaces, then a frustrated hand slamming a desk lightly. | Voiceover: “Turns out, vague guidelines lead to very confused AI. And a lot of wasted time.”

Scene 3 | Visual: A single, clear sticky note on a desk reads: “Double-Check Your Guidelines!” | Voiceover: “Always ensure your annotation rules are crystal clear.”

Closing line: “Invest in clear guidelines – your data, and your sanity, will thank you.”
