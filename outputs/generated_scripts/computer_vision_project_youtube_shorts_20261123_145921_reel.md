# Reel Script: Computer vision project (YouTube Shorts)

Hook (0-3s): “Mind. Blown.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up shot of a blurry, slightly out-of-focus photo of a simple grey cat. | Voiceover: “Here’s a picture… completely ordinary.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Timelapse – the grey cat photo rapidly transforms, layers of detail appearing, color blooming, the image becoming incredibly realistic and detailed. AI generation is visibly happening. | Voiceover: “But what if it wasn’t?”

Scene 3 | Visual: The final, stunning AI-generated image of the cat – vibrant, perfectly lit, in a beautiful outdoor setting, looking directly at the camera. | Voiceover: “Computer vision is changing everything.”

Closing line: “Explore the future of art – watch more!”
