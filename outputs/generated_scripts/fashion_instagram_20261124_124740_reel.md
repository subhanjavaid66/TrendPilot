# Reel Script: Fashion (Instagram)

Hook (0-3s): “Daring is the new chic.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Quick cuts – a person dramatically throws a silk scarf around their neck, then pulls out a bright yellow blazer, then adds chunky boots. The transitions are fast and energetic.
| Voiceover: “We’ve all had those moments. The ones that felt… electric.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Slow-motion shot of someone confidently walking down a street in a layered, unexpected outfit – perhaps a floral dress with a leather jacket and bold accessories.
| Voiceover: “It’s about injecting your personality, pushing boundaries, and creating a look that’s *you*.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Close-up on a smiling face, wearing a playfully mismatched outfit.
| Voiceover: “So, what’s *your* most daring style moment? Share it in the comments!”

Closing line: “Let’s celebrate the looks that break the rules.”
