# Reel Script: Model failed in production (Instagram)

Hook (0-3s): “Seriously?”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up shot of a perfectly styled model mid-pose, frozen in a slightly awkward expression. Dramatic, slightly unsettling music plays. | Voiceover: “We’ve all seen it. The ‘effortless’ perfection.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick cuts of various production fails – a prop falling, a lighting glitch, a wardrobe malfunction – all sped up and slightly chaotic. | Voiceover: “But let’s be real. Beautiful faces don't automatically mean smooth shoots.”

Scene 3 | Visual: The original model from Scene 1 now looking exasperated, tilting her head, with a slightly sheepish smile. | Voiceover: “Sometimes, even the most stunning models face… hiccups.”

Closing line: “Don’t let the highlight reel fool you. Let’s celebrate the real process.”
