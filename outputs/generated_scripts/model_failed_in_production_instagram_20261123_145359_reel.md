# Reel Script: Model failed in production (Instagram)

Hook (0-3s): “Production down? Seriously?”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up of a model’s panicked face, sweat dripping, staring at a blank screen. Dramatic, slightly distorted music playing. | Voiceover: “You’ve practiced, you’re ready… the lighting’s wrong.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick cuts – a stressed stage manager frantically pointing, a technician fiddling with cables, a monitor displaying a flashing error message. | Voiceover: “It's not the audience judging you... it’s the tech.”

Scene 3 | Visual: The model dramatically throws their hands up in the air, looking utterly defeated. Slow zoom out. | Voiceover: “That’s the *real* stage fright.”

Closing line: “Don’t let a glitch steal your moment. Level up your production prep!”
