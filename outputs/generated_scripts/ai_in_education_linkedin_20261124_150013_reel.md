# Reel Script: AI in Education (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “AI in education – it’s here, but what’s *really* holding us back?”

Scene 1 | Visual: Split screen – one side shows a diverse group of educators thoughtfully discussing, the other shows a stylized graphic of a student receiving personalized feedback from an AI tutor.
| Voiceover: “The potential of AI in education is undeniable – personalized learning pathways, targeted feedback… but many educators face a key challenge.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Close-up shot of an educator’s hand thoughtfully considering a tablet displaying an AI-generated report.
| Voiceover: “Currently, we’re seeing AI tools assist with tasks like student writing, but integrating them effectively requires addressing a significant hurdle: equitable access and thoughtful implementation.”

Scene 3 | Visual: The group of educators from Scene 1 now pointing to the screen, engaging in a discussion.
| Voiceover: “What’s the biggest obstacle *you* foresee when considering AI in your setting? Let’s discuss strategies for responsible integration and maximizing impact. Share your thoughts below!”

Closing line: “Let’s build the future of learning, together.”
