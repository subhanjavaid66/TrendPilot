# Reel Script: Data annotation struggles (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “We were drowning in pixels.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Rapid montage of people intensely labeling images on computers, increasingly stressed expressions. | Voiceover: “Our team was tasked with annotating millions of images. We were fueled by coffee and…desperation.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Close-up shot of a single, slightly blurry image. A hand hesitantly circles a small, ambiguous shape. | Voiceover: “After 72 hours, we hadn’t identified a single object. Just… a deep, unsettling appreciation for the sheer volume of ambiguous data.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Slow zoom out to show the team slumped over their desks, looking defeated but strangely calm. | Voiceover: “Turns out, some problems are best left unsolved.”

Closing line: “Let’s talk about smarter annotation solutions. Reach out if you’re facing a similar mountain of data.”
