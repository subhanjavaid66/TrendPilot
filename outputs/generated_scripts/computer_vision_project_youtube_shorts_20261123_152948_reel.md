# Reel Script: Computer vision project (YouTube Shorts)

Hook (0-3s): Ever wondered how robots “see”?

Scene 1 | Visual: Rapid cuts of a computer screen displaying object detection – a cat, a car, a tree – highlighting with bounding boxes. | Voiceover: “It’s called computer vision, and it’s basically teaching computers to *see*!”

Scene 2 | Visual: Close-up of a camera feed analyzing a street scene, identifying pedestrians and traffic lights. | Voiceover: “We’re feeding algorithms data, letting them learn patterns – just like we do.”

Scene 3 | Visual: A split screen – one side shows a human looking, the other shows a computer analyzing the same image. | Voiceover: “Suddenly, machines can recognize objects, understand scenes… it’s seriously mind-blowing!”

Closing line: “Explore the future of AI – like what *it* sees!”
