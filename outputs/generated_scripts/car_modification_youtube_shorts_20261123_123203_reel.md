# Reel Script: Car modification (YouTube Shorts)

Hook (0-3s): Unleash the beast!

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up shot of a classic Mustang, looking stock. | Voiceover: “This Mustang was… fine.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Rapid cuts of the car being modified – wheels being swapped, exhaust system installed, spoiler attached, engine bay work. Fast-paced music plays. | Voiceover: “But we weren’t done. Not by a long shot!”

Scene 3 | Visual: The finished Mustang roaring down a road, looking aggressive and powerful. | Voiceover: “Now? This is a force to be reckoned with!”

Closing line: “Subscribe for more wild car builds! 🔥”
