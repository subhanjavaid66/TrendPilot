# Reel Script: Model failed in production (Instagram)

Hook (0-3s): “Okay, let’s do this.”

Scene 1 | Visual: POV – Close up on perfectly styled hair, flawless makeup, a stunning outfit. A hand delicately adjusts a necklace. | Voiceover: “Just finished my biggest shoot yet. Feeling *amazing*.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick cut to the camera rolling. The stylist frantically adjusts the lighting. The model subtly grimaces. A rogue pigeon flies into view. | Voiceover: “Then… chaos. Like, *serious* chaos.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Montage of increasingly ridiculous fails – a sudden gust of wind, a dropped prop, a startled reaction from the model.  Fast cuts, slightly sped up. | Voiceover: “My face. My outfit. My *dreams*… gone.”

Closing line: “Anyone else ever feel like this? Let me know in the comments!”
