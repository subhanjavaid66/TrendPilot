# Reel Script: Fashion (Instagram)

Hook (0-3s): “Fast fashion doesn’t have to mean fast *out*.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up shot of a hand gently touching a beautifully textured, durable fabric – perhaps linen or a high-quality cotton.
| Voiceover: “For too long, ‘fast fashion’ has been linked to poor quality. But that’s changing.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Quick montage – skilled artisan working with fabric, a close-up of a garment being meticulously stitched, then a slow pan across a well-made, timeless outfit.
| Voiceover: “Innovative techniques and skilled artisans are transforming materials into lasting staples.”

Scene 3 | Visual: Return to the hand gently touching the fabric, now with a shot of a stylish, confident individual wearing the garment.
| Voiceover: “It’s about investing in pieces designed to last, redefining a stylish wardrobe.”

Closing line: “Choose consciously. Build enduring style.”
