# Reel Script: Data annotation struggles (LinkedIn)

Hook (0-3s): “Data annotation: it’s… exhausting.”

Scene 1 | Visual: A person dramatically slumped over a computer, surrounded by sticky notes and coffee cups. | Voiceover: “You spend hours meticulously labeling images, feeling like a digital detective.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Close-up of a single training image subtly altered – a slightly different angle, a tiny change in lighting. | Voiceover: “Then… BAM! Suddenly, all your work is gone. Because one tiny change throws everything off.”

Scene 3 | Visual: The person from Scene 1 throws their hands up in mock despair, then shrugs with a slightly bewildered smile. | Voiceover: “Seriously, what’s *your* biggest data annotation struggle?”

Closing line: “Let’s talk about it. Share your frustrations in the comments!”
