# Reel Script: Computer vision project (YouTube Shorts)

Hook (0-3s): “Blurry to brilliant!”

Scene 1 | Visual: Close-up of a completely blurry, dark photo of a flower. | Voiceover: “This was… well, a mess.”

Scene 2 | Visual: The blurry photo smoothly transitions, pixel by pixel, into a crisp, high-resolution image of the same flower, bathed in sunlight. Speed up the transformation. | Voiceover: “But wait… what if we could fix it?”

Scene 3 | Visual: The stunning, detailed image of the flower remains on screen, slowly zooming out to reveal a computer screen with lines of code briefly flashing. | Voiceover: “That’s the power of computer vision – instantly revealing hidden detail.”

Closing line: “Want to see more amazing transformations? Subscribe now!”
