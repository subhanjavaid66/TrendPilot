# Reel Script: Python learning journey (Instagram)

Hook (0-3s): “My Python journey? It’s… intense.”

Scene 1 | Visual: Rapid montage of someone frantically typing on a laptop, eyes wide with confusion, interspersed with error messages flashing on the screen. | Voiceover: “I started with ‘Hello, World!’ and now I’m staring into the abyss of indentation errors.”

Scene 2 | Visual: Close-up of a hand dramatically throwing a rubber duck at the screen. | Voiceover: “I’ve tried debugging with a rubber duck. It just stares back. Unhelpful duck.”

Scene 3 | Visual: The person from Scene 1 slumped in a chair, looking utterly defeated, then a hopeful smile appears as they open a tutorial. | Voiceover: “Seriously, someone please send tutorials… and maybe a therapist.”

Closing line: “Let me know if you’ve conquered the Python maze – I need all the help I can get!”
